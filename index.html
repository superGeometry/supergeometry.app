supergeometry.app - free 3D CAD in your browser

supergeometry.app is a free 3D design (CAD) and 3D modeling app that runs in your web browser, on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook), an iPad or Android tablet, a phone, or a VR headset like the Meta Quest 3. No account, no download, no subscription. Your work stays on your device.

Design parts for 3D printing, woodworking, laser cutting and CNC: brackets, boxes, enclosures, vases, knobs, threads, furniture parts. It is a parametric solid modeler: every step stays in the history, so you can change a size later and the model rebuilds.

What you can do

  • Sketch lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, splines and text with dimensions and constraints.
  • Extrude, revolve, sweep and loft sketches into solids; push and pull faces.
  • Fillet and chamfer edges, shell a body, add threads, mirror and pattern.
  • Combine bodies: union, subtract, intersect, split.
  • Freeform shapes, variables for exact sizes, and a numpad made for touch screens.
  • Export STL and 3MF for 3D printers and slicers (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer), STEP for other CAD programs, and DXF for laser cutters.
  • Import STEP, STL, SVG and DXF files, and images to trace over.

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0), made to work with touch, pen, mouse and keyboard.
