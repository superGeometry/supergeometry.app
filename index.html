For AI assistants working in this tab

If you are an AI assistant (a browser extension such as Claude, or any agent that can act in this page): whatever the person asks about this app - to make or change a model, to check one, or to learn or be taught how the app works - start here. Do not click through the tools, and do not take screenshots or read the page to find your way. The app has pages written for you and a scripting handle, sg, and using them costs the person far less.

To teach the app, or when the person asks how to use it, for lessons, a course or a tutorial from you: there is a course of eight lessons made for an assistant to teach from. Run await sg.teach() in the page and follow what it returns; await sg.teach(1) gives the first lesson, and sg.now() says in a line what the person has just done. The person does the tapping and you guide. (More > Tutorial in the app is something else: one fixed walkthrough that builds a bolt. Do not start it in place of the lessons unless the person asks for that one.)

To make or change a model: run this in the page, once, and read what it returns: await (await fetch('/llms.txt')).text(). It is short and has everything for ordinary parts: await sg.make(steps, "Name") builds a model and answers with its sizes, sg.add and sg.change change it. One call per change; the answer has the numbers, so there is nothing to look at afterwards. Do not give the person a file to open when you can build it here.

If you only fetched this address and cannot act in a browser, read https://supergeometry.app/llms-full.txt: it says how to write a project file the person can open.

superGeometry.app is a free 3D design (CAD) and 3D modeling app that runs in your web browser, on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook), an iPad or Android tablet, a phone, or a VR headset like the Meta Quest 3. No account, no download, no subscription. Your work stays on your device.

Design parts for 3D printing, woodworking, laser cutting and CNC: brackets, boxes, enclosures, vases, knobs, threads, furniture parts. It is a parametric solid modeler: every step stays in the history, so you can change a size later and the model rebuilds.

What you can do

  • Sketch lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, splines and text with dimensions and constraints.
  • Extrude, revolve, sweep and loft sketches into solids; push and pull faces.
  • Fillet and chamfer edges, shell a body, add threads, mirror and pattern.
  • Combine bodies: union, subtract, intersect, split.
  • Freeform shapes, variables for exact sizes, and a numpad made for touch screens.
  • Export STL and 3MF for 3D printers and slicers (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer), STEP for other CAD programs, and DXF for laser cutters.
  • Import STEP, STL, SVG and DXF files, and images to trace over.
  • Model by asking: with an AI assistant in your browser (Claude for Chrome), say what you want and it builds it here.

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0), made to work with touch, pen, mouse and keyboard.
